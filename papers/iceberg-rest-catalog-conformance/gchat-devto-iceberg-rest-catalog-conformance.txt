Mine is submitted. Every vendor with a lakehouse now ships an Iceberg REST catalog and each says it implements the same specification, so I pointed one identical request suite at seven of them on 2026-09-03: BigLake, AWS Glue, AWS S3 Tables, Databricks Unity, Snowflake Horizon, Microsoft OneLake, and a self-hosted Apache Polaris as the control.
It covers 25 of the specification's 35 operations, and checks three things: whether an endpoint is served, 30 loadTable field paths, and whether the endpoints array a catalog publishes about itself matches what it actually serves. Eleven endpoints are declared and not served across four vendors, and only the open-source control declares accurately. Two of the seven publish no declaration at all, so a client cannot discover their surface without probing it. Views are served by one catalog of the seven. And 24 of 30 loadTable field paths are identical everywhere, which is a null result and worth saying plainly.
The harness and the anonymised evidence are in the repo, so the verdicts are re-derivable without re-running against a vendor: https://github.com/xbill9/lakehouse-iceberg-2026
